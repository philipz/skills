# Cart Abandonment Recovery — User Stories for an E-Commerce Platform

## Problem/Feature Description

ShopFlow, a mid-market e-commerce platform serving specialty retailers, has identified that roughly 68% of shoppers who add items to their cart never complete a purchase. The product team wants to address this with a cart abandonment recovery feature: a combination of automated email reminders, in-app nudges, and optional discount incentives that re-engage shoppers who leave without buying.

The feature must handle multiple cart states (guest vs. registered user, items still in stock vs. out of stock), support configurable reminder timelines, allow merchants to toggle discount incentives on or off, and comply with email marketing opt-out regulations. The customer success team, marketing managers, and individual retailers are all invested in how this works.

The engineering team works in two-week Agile sprints. The product owner needs a set of user stories that are ready to be loaded into the sprint backlog and are actionable without further clarification.

## Output Specification

Produce the following files in your working directory:

- `user_stories.md` — A set of at least 5 user stories for the cart abandonment recovery feature, each following a standard user story structure and including acceptance criteria and required story metadata
- `backlog_summary.md` — A brief table listing each story ID, its title, and its priority classification, suitable for sprint planning

Each story should be self-contained enough for a developer to begin implementation without needing clarification from the product owner.
