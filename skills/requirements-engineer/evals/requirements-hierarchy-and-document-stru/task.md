# Loan Origination Portal — Requirements Specification

## Problem/Feature Description

FinBridge, a mid-size fintech startup, is building a new digital loan origination portal that will allow customers to apply for personal and business loans entirely online. Currently all loan applications are handled through branch offices using paper forms, creating a bottleneck that caps growth and frustrates customers who expect a modern, self-service experience.

The product team has done preliminary discovery: they know the portal needs to support identity verification, credit checking, document upload, loan decisioning, and status tracking. The CTO and CFO are the primary decision-makers. Customer-facing staff, compliance officers, and the core banking integration team are key stakeholders. The portal must comply with relevant consumer lending regulations.

Your job is to produce a complete, structured requirements specification for this portal — starting from business objectives and working down to system-level requirements. The output should be detailed enough that a development team could begin architectural planning from it.

## Output Specification

Produce the following files in your working directory:

- `brs.md` — Business Requirements Specification covering business objectives, stakeholders, in-scope/out-of-scope boundaries, business requirements, risks, and assumptions
- `srs.md` — System/Software Requirements Specification covering functional requirements, non-functional requirements (at least performance and security), interface requirements, and a requirements traceability matrix linking system requirements back to business requirements
- `requirements_summary.md` — A brief summary listing all requirement IDs generated, their level (Business/Stakeholder/System), and their priority

The specifications should be detailed enough to demonstrate the full requirements hierarchy from business goals down to individual system behaviors.
